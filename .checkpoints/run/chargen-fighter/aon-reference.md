## 2. Backgrounds (Player Core)

All Player Core backgrounds follow the pattern: two attribute boosts — one constrained to a choice
of two listed attributes, one free — plus one trained skill, one Lore skill, and one skill feat.

> **NOTE — "Blacksmith":** No background named "Blacksmith" exists on AoN (searched all sources,
> legacy and remaster). The Player Core background matching a blacksmith concept is **Artisan**
> ([/Backgrounds.aspx?ID=409](https://2e.aonprd.com/Backgrounds.aspx?ID=409), Player Core pg. 84 —
> "You might have been a blacksmith's apprentice toiling over the forge"):
> boosts: one to **Strength or Intelligence** + one free; trained in **Crafting**; **Guild Lore**;
> skill feat **Specialty Crafting**. If hand-authored data contains a "Blacksmith" background, it is
> not authentic Player Core content and should either be renamed Artisan or flagged as homebrew.

**Level-1 class features:** Ancestry and background; attribute boosts (four free boosts during
character creation); initial proficiencies; **Reactive Strike**; **fighter feat** (one 1st-level
class feat); **Shield Block**.

- **Reactive Strike** (remaster name for legacy "Attack of Opportunity") — class feature (pg. 136)
  grants the Reactive Strike reaction
  ([/Actions.aspx?ID=2256](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2256), pg. 138): *Trigger* — a
  creature within your reach uses a manipulate action or a move action, makes a ranged attack, or
  leaves a square during a move action. Make a melee Strike against the triggering creature; crit
  hit disrupts a triggering manipulate action; the Strike neither counts toward nor suffers your
  multiple attack penalty.
- **Shield Block** — gain the Shield Block general feat
  ([/Feats.aspx?ID=5212](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=5212), pg. 262): Reaction; *Trigger* —
  while your shield is raised, you would take physical (B/P/S) damage from an attack. Shield
  prevents damage up to its Hardness; you and the shield each take remaining damage.

## 6. Currency and Bulk Basics

- **Starting wealth**: "Characters begin play with **15 gp (or 150 sp)** to spend on equipment."
  — Currency, [/Rules.aspx?ID=3295](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=3295) (Player Core pg. 10).
- **Coin values**: 1 gp = 10 sp = 100 cp; 1 pp = 10 gp. The silver piece is the standard unit.
  — Coins and Currency, [/Rules.aspx?ID=2144](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2144) (Player Core pg. 267).
- **Light (L) items**: 10 light items make up 1 Bulk. (Fractions of 10 light items round down for
  the total.) — Bulk, [/Rules.aspx?ID=2054](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2054) (Player Core pg. 27).
- **Encumbered threshold**: You can carry Bulk equal to **5 + your Strength modifier** without
  penalty; exceeding that gives the **encumbered** condition (clumsy 1 and a –10-foot penalty to
  all Speeds, which can't reduce Speed below 5 feet). You **cannot** carry more than
  **10 + Strength modifier** Bulk. — Bulk Limits,
  [/Rules.aspx?ID=2154](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2154) (Player Core pg. 269); also
  [/Rules.aspx?ID=2054](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2054) (pg. 27).
