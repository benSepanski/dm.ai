## Summary

Full 8-phase scenario completed end-to-end through the **in-app browser** with
no blocking issues. The narrative engine is a standout: every AI turn stayed on
setting, referenced the actual PCs, and maintained tight continuity (the
seven-year cycle threaded from hook → dialogue → combat loot → aftermath → stored
summary), with durable inventions correctly routed through proposal cards. The
character wizard and combat tracker are solid on the mechanical basics —
live-computed scores, guardrails that block illegal state, clamped monster
stats, correct 2024 to-hit math, dice-backed system messages, and server-side
action-economy enforcement (409 + clear message). The four findings cluster into
two themes: **combat fidelity** — a wizard cannot cast in combat (PT-28) and the
Attack action falls back to an unarmed strike instead of the equipped weapon
(PT-29), so weapons, damage types, spells, and weapon masteries never reach the
table; and **character-build completeness** — the shield toggle doesn't affect
AC (PT-27) and Human's Versatile origin feat is silently dropped (PT-30). None
blocked the run. One environment caveat: react-konva token drag isn't drivable
by the in-app browser's synthetic input, so drag/mirroring needs a human pass.
